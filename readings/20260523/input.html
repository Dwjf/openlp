{su}14:13 {/su}When Jesus heard this, he withdrew from there in a boat to a deserted place by himself. When the crowds heard it, they followed him on foot from the towns. {su}14:13 {/su}Apabila Yesus mendengar berita tentang Yohanes, Dia meninggalkan tempat itu dengan perahu, lalu pergi sendirian ke suatu tempat yang sunyi. Tetapi orang ramai mendengar hal itu, lalu mereka meninggalkan bandar mereka dan mengikut Yesus melalui jalan darat. [===] {su}14:14 {/su}When he went ashore he saw a great crowd, and he had compassion on them and healed their sick. {su}14:14 {/su}Apabila Yesus turun dari perahu dan melihat orang ramai itu, Dia mengasihani mereka dan menyembuhkan orang sakit di situ. [===] {su}14:15 {/su}When evening came, the disciples came to him and said, "This is a deserted place, and the hour is now late. Send the crowds away to go into the villages and buy food for themselves." {su}14:15 {/su}Pada petang harinya, pengikut-pengikut Yesus datang dan berkata kepada-Nya, "Hari sudah hampir malam dan tempat ini terpencil. Suruhlah semua orang ini pergi, supaya dapat membeli makanan di pekan." [===] {su}14:16 {/su}But Jesus said, "They need not go away; you give them something to eat." {su}14:16 {/su}Tetapi Yesus menjawab, "Mereka tidak usah pergi; kamu sendiri beri mereka makanan." [===] {su}14:17 {/su}They said to him, "We have only five loaves here and two fish." {su}14:17 {/su}Pengikut-pengikut-Nya berkata, "Kami hanya mempunyai lima ketul roti dan dua ekor ikan!" [===] {su}14:18 {/su}And he said, "Bring them here to me." {su}14:18 {/su}Yesus berkata, "Bawalah makanan itu kepada-Ku." [===] {su}14:19 {/su}Then he ordered the crowds to sit down on the grass, and taking the five loaves and the two fish, he looked up to heaven and gave thanks. Then he broke the loaves and gave them to the disciples, and the disciples gave them to the crowds. {su}14:19 {/su}Kemudian Dia menyuruh orang ramai duduk di atas rumput. Lalu Yesus mengambil lima ketul roti dan dua ekor ikan itu. Kemudian Dia menengadah ke langit dan mengucap syukur kepada Allah. Setelah itu Dia membahagikan roti itu dan menyerahkannya kepada pengikut-pengikut-Nya supaya diberikan kepada orang ramai itu. [===] {su}14:20 {/su}And they all ate and were satisfied. And they took up twelve baskets full of the broken pieces left over. {su}14:20 {/su}Tiap-tiap orang makan sehingga kenyang. Selepas itu pengikut-pengikut-Nya mengumpulkan lebihan makanan sebanyak dua belas buah bakul. [===] {su}14:21 {/su}And those who ate were about five thousand men, besides women and children. {su}14:21 {/su}Jumlah orang lelaki yang makan kira-kira lima ribu orang, belum terhitung wanita dan kanak-kanak.
