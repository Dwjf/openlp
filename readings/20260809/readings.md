# Saturday of the 7th Week of Easter — 23 May 2026

Source: https://bible.usccb.org/bible/readings/052326.cfm

## First Reading — Acts 28:16-20, 30-31
Paul arrives in Rome under guard and addresses Jewish leaders about his
imprisonment, stating "I wear these chains" due to "the hope of Israel."
He spends two years proclaiming the Kingdom of God.

## Responsorial Psalm — Psalm 11:4, 5, 7
Response: "The just will gaze on your face, O Lord" (or "Alleluia")

## Gospel Acclamation — John 16:7, 13
"I will send to you the Spirit of truth... he will guide you to all truth."

## Gospel — John 21:20-25
Peter asks Jesus about the disciple whom Jesus loved. Jesus responds with
"What concern is it of yours?" regarding that disciple's future, then
instructs Peter to "Follow me." The passage concludes noting countless other
deeds of Jesus exist.

---
Note: This is for the morning Mass. If preparing for the evening **Vigil of Pentecost**, the readings are different (extended OT options + Rom 8:22–27 + John 7:37–39).
